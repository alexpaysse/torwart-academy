What Every Session Covers

No two athletes are the same. Sessions are structured around your current level and adapted as you grow — but these are the pillars every keeper trains on.

🧤

Shot Stopping

Technique for low balls, driven shots, headers, and aerial claiming. Repetition that builds real muscle memory under pressure.

📐

Positioning & Angles

How to read an attack before it develops — where to set up, how to cut down angles, and how to command your box with authority.

⚽

Distribution

Goal kicks, drop kicks, punt kicks, overarm throws, and quick restarts. Starting attacks from the back with purpose and accuracy.

🔁

1v1 & Breakaways

Reading the attacker, closing down at the right moment, body shape, and making yourself big to win those crucial one-on-one situations.

🧠

Decision Making

When to come, when to stay, when to punch vs. catch. Building the mental game — reading the field and making fast, confident calls.

💪

Footwork & Athleticism

Lateral quickness, explosive first steps, set position, and the movement patterns that separate good keepers from great ones.

📣

Communication

Organizing your back line, calling for the ball, directing pressure — the verbal leadership skills that make a keeper a true captain.

🎯

Set Piece Defense

Corners, free kicks, and penalty situations. Setup, positioning, claiming crosses, and staying composed when the game is on the line.

⚡

Reactions & Reflex

Fast-twitch training to sharpen your instincts — deflections off the wall, rebound situations, and close-range reaction saves.
