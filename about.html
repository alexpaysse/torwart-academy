About the Trainer

Alex Paysse

Born and raised in Brainerd, Minnesota, Alex Paysse has spent his life mastering the art of goalkeeping — from the fields of central MN to competitive ECNL pitches and the semi-pro stage.


Alex competes for St. Croix Soccer Club — one of the top clubs in the Midwest. At the club level he plays for the St. Croix 09 Boys in the ECNL (Elite Clubs National League) — the most competitive youth soccer platform in the United States, featuring the best club teams and players in the country.


Beyond the club game, Alex recently made his semi-pro debut with St. Croix Legends in the UPSL (United Premier Soccer League), stepping onto the professional pathway while still competing at the elite youth level.


Torwart Academy was built on a simple idea: goalkeepers deserve specialized, high-level training rooted in real philosophy. "Torwart" — German for goalkeeper — reflects a commitment to the technical precision and mental discipline that defines world-class shot-stoppers.
